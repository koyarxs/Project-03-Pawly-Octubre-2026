Created with the built-in image_gen tool for the requested appointment-section composition. These are presentation assets. The calendar and service panel are now a static illustration; the only action is the link to the full booking page.

## companions.png

Transparent photograph-style golden retriever and tabby cat cutout.

Prompt: "Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: transparent cutout for a veterinary clinic website. Create a highly photorealistic full-body golden retriever sitting facing the camera, happy relaxed open mouth, behind and slightly to the left of a seated brown-gray tabby and white domestic cat facing the camera. The dog is about twice as tall as the cat; the cat sits closely nestled at the dog's front-right paws. Natural anatomy, realistic golden fur and whiskers, warm soft daylight. Both pets fully visible including ears, tails and paws, compact vertical grouping, front-facing eye-level photography. NO floor, NO furniture, NO oval or background, NO mat, NO text, NO collar logos, NO UI. Genuinely transparent alpha background around both animals. Composition like a premium veterinary advertising photo: dog taller on left, cat lower on right. Output portrait 3:4 composition with minimal transparent margins."
